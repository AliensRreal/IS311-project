Web Weavers is a student club that specializes in web development. Our goal is to share our knowledge and expertise in the aspect of web development.

Web Weavers was founded by Information System students who were passionate about web development, they transformed their passion into something bigger in hopes of sharing the knowledge in web development.

Our team at Web Weavers is going to host events in the university the shares information about web developments, in addition we will host competitions to increase the skills of the student in web development.
